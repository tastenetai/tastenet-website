Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later? +
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any differences.
Do you offer free trials? +
Yes! TASTE JUDGE offers a 14-day free trial with 1,000 assessments. For TASTE SENTINEL, we offer personalized demos to qualified prospects.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and purchase orders for Enterprise plans. Annual plans receive a 15% discount.
Is there a setup fee? +
No setup fees for TASTE SENTINEL or TASTE JUDGE. TASTE VISION has a one-time design fee as listed in the pricing.
Can I get a custom quote? +
Absolutely! Contact our sales team for custom enterprise solutions, volume discounts, or special requirements.
